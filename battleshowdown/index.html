Overview

BattleShowdown: Gambit was a commercially released 2D competitive multiplayer platform shooter for iOS and Android developed by Ethlas.

The game reworked and re-released an earlier Ethlas title, Battle Showdown, combining fast-paced platform combat with character customisation, progression systems, multiplayer game modes, and integrations with external NFT collections and Web3 services.

I worked on the project from the beginning of its redevelopment, initially as a Game Artist while also contributing to game design and visual direction. Following a company restructure in December 2023, I transitioned into the role of Game Producer and took ownership of its design and production responsibilities.

From January 2024 onward, I coordinated development for a distributed team of one full-time artist and two contracted developers in Vietnam; defined priorities; designed gameplay and progression systems; directed visual development; reviewed implementations; managed partner requirements; conducted playtests; and prepared weekly releases. The game remained active until July 28, 2024, when I left Ethlas to begin university.

Project Design Goals

  • Build a responsive and engaging competitive mobile platform shooter for short multiplayer sessions.
  • Establish a consistent visual identity across original characters and characters adapted from external IP and NFT collections.
  • Create readable, intuitive mobile UI/UX for competitive gameplay and progression-heavy systems.
  • Design progression and reward systems that encouraged repeated play without overwhelming core combat.
  • Provide customisation options within a shared combat framework.
  • Support frequent live-service updates, seasonal content, partnerships, and limited-time modes.
  • Maintain a pipeline for a small distributed team to deliver new content weekly.

Production & Team Leadership

As Game Producer, I coordinated both design and production for BattleShowdown: Gambit.

  • Managed one full-time artist and two overseas contract developers; assigned tasks and set weekly priorities.
  • Planned milestones, organised release schedules, and translated business, design, and partner requirements into development tasks.
  • Coordinated art, engineering, management, and external partners; reviewed work before implementation and release.
  • Provided visual, gameplay, and UX feedback; managed scope; and prioritised bugs, features, live-service content, and partner deliverables.
  • Maintained a weekly update cadence, led internal and external feedback sessions, and iterated systems from playtesting and player feedback.

Working in a reduced startup team required constant movement between production, game design, art direction, UI/UX, balancing, implementation, and partner management.

Game & Systems Design

I contributed to design from the beginning and assumed primary ownership of the game's systems after becoming Game Producer.

  • Designed and iterated multiplayer gameplay systems, and adjusted weapon values and statistics to improve combat balance.
  • Designed character attributes and equippable passive attributes, enabling customisation without changing the shared core ability set.
  • Balanced character archetypes around broad roles: Tanky, Agile, and Versatile.
  • Reworked currencies, XP, player progression, unlock progression, match rewards, character acquisition, and reward pacing.
  • Iterated progression using playtests and player feedback, balancing competitive play with long-term collection goals.

A major challenge was giving a large roster of visually distinct characters meaningful gameplay customisation while keeping combat understandable and manageable for a small team.

Game Modes & Level Design

  • Designed a Capture the Flag mode and map for a BNB collaboration.
  • Designed a limited-time Christmas Jump Quest, a 2v2 Halloween event map, an unreleased 2v2 cyberpunk map, and an unreleased map for sister-company title PokPok.
  • Created layouts, gameplay spaces, platform placement, visual themes, combat routes, and traversal opportunities.
  • Created final visual assets for several maps and set up completed maps in Unity for developer integration.
  • Edited colliders and gameplay boundaries, and prepared reusable Unity map prefabs.

This work covered both level design and environment production, from initial layout concepts through visual production and engine setup.

Weapons & Combat Content

  • Designed the visual identity and final assets for more than 40 weapons.
  • Created weapon concepts, projectile artwork, weapon VFX, and frame-by-frame projectile and combat animations.
  • Built selected effects using the Unity Particle System.
  • Assisted with numerical weapon balancing and adjusted values through internal and external playtests.
  • Iterated combat readability and visual feedback for mobile screen constraints and weapon differentiation.

Character Design & Customisation

The game mixed original characters with characters from external collaborations. For partner characters, I adapted supplied references into BattleShowdown's established visual style.

  • Designed original playable characters, adapted external IP and NFT characters, and maintained consistent proportions and visual language.
  • Created accessories, cosmetics, character role identities, and production-ready character assets.
  • Prepared separated head, body, and leg assets for the custom prefab animation system.
  • Created and reviewed character animation assets, differentiating characters through roles, stats, cosmetics, and configurable passive attributes.

UI / UX Design

I was responsible for the overall UI/UX design and application flow; the additional artist primarily produced UI assets from my layouts, direction, and feedback.

  • Designed every major screen, wireframes, Figma mockups, user flows, navigation structures, information hierarchy, menus, and transitions.
  • Designed the mobile HUD, progression and reward interfaces, character-selection and customisation flows, currencies, inventory, and Web3-related interfaces.
  • Reviewed implementations, produced selected UI assets and icons, and ensured readability across mobile screen sizes.

The interface communicated characters, equipment, progression, rewards, currencies, and connected Web3 features while remaining usable on a mobile device.

Progression & Reward Systems

I reworked the metagame, including currencies, XP, unlock progression, match rewards, character rewards, progression pacing, character acquisition, and reward balancing.

The goal was a clearer loop where multiplayer matches meaningfully unlocked content and developed a player's collection. I continuously adjusted values based on playtest data, player feedback, and observed progression behaviour.

Art Direction & Visual Development

I directed the overall visual identity using a stylised 2D vector-art approach across characters, environments, weapons, accessories, effects, icons, and interface elements.

  • Established and maintained visual direction; created characters, environments, maps, weapons, cosmetics, icons, interface assets, animations, VFX, and projectiles.
  • Directed the additional artist, reviewed visual assets before implementation, and ensured external collaboration characters fit the established style.
  • Created or directed production of more than one hundred game assets.
  • Collaborated with Ethlas' marketing lead and freelance artists on advertisements, campaign assets, and promotional trailers.

Animation & Unity Implementation

While not responsible for gameplay programming, I worked in Unity to prepare and integrate art and gameplay content.

  • Set up completed levels, reusable map prefabs, colliders, gameplay boundaries, and visual assets.
  • Created selected Unity Particle System effects and 2D frame-by-frame animations.
  • Prepared separated character artwork for the custom animation-prefab system and reviewed visual implementations in-engine.

Live-Service Development & Playtesting

The team released a new build or content update every week.

  • Planned weekly updates, ran weekly internal playtests, organised biweekly structured feedback sessions, and gathered external-player feedback.
  • Prioritised recurring usability and gameplay issues; adjusted balance values, UI flows, progression, rewards, maps, and modes between builds.
  • Worked with an overseas gaming guild for external testing. At gamescom asia 2023 in Singapore, I gathered player feedback and invited new players for user interviews on onboarding, gameplay, and usability.

Partnerships & External Collaborations

I coordinated with overseas partners to adapt their characters and requirements into suitable game content. Collaborations included BNB, Beast Kingdom / Ultraman Blazar, BattleFly, Azuki, CFC, GodJira, and other Web3 and NFT communities.

  • Communicated with representatives, interpreted character references, designed characters in the game's style, and coordinated deadlines.
  • Prepared event assets, promotional and reward deliverables, and ensured content met internal and partner expectations.
  • For Beast Kingdom, the campaign incorporated Ultraman Blazar content alongside physical promotional merchandise.

Web3 Systems

The game included wallet connections, NFT ownership verification, character ownership integrations, marketplace connections through Gambit.fi, and Player Shares. Player Shares let users allocate digital assets toward participating players, with outcomes tied to performance.

My role focused on the game design, UI/UX, player-facing flows, visual presentation, and integration requirements rather than underlying blockchain implementation.
