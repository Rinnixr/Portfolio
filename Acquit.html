Acquit

A Single-Player Courtroom Strategy Card Game

Acquit is a physical role-playing strategy card game where players take on the role of a defence attorney preparing and presenting a case in court.
Build your case during the pre-trial phase by investigating, gathering evidence, and preparing tactics before entering the courtroom, where your choices directly influence your chances of countering each argument.
Plan your case carefully, manage your limited resources, and persuade the court to secure an acquittal.

Design Rationale

Acquit was designed around translating the process of preparing and arguing a legal case into a strategic card game. To represent this, we divided the game into two distinct phases: Pre-Trial and Trial.

During Pre-Trial, players use Investigation cards to build their collection of Evidence and Tactics. This creates a preparation and deck-building stage where the player must consider what resources may be useful later. During the Trial, these resources are then used alongside a chosen Motion to counter Arguments, modifying dice rolls and giving the player additional ways to respond to each situation.

The combination of hand-building, dice rolls, evidence matching, argument difficulty and set bonuses was designed to create a balance between preparation, decision-making and uncertainty. Rather than relying entirely on luck, players can improve their odds through the decisions they make before and during the trial.


For this project, I took primary responsibility for developing the game's core systems and mechanics as part of a 6-person team. I designed the interactions between the different card types, structured the scoring systems, and worked extensively on balancing and playtesting the game. I also contributed to refining the overall player flow and authored the rules required to turn the concept into a complete and playable physical card game.


No AI-generated artwork was used in this project. I was responsible for the overall visual design and produced the majority of the game's assets, with additional assets created by my teammates.
